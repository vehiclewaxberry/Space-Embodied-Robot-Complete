# B51 contact pad and preload trade

Status: `TRADE_OPEN_NO_PRELOAD_OR_CONTACT_QUALIFICATION`

| Option | Function | Current evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Replaceable compliant pad | tolerance take-up and local pressure control | candidate pad geometry only | HOLD |
| Hard machined seat with shim | deterministic datum and service replacement | no tolerance stack or surface specification | HOLD |
| Strap or clamp preload | vertical capture | no load, routing or release hardware | HOLD |
| Dedicated HDRM preload | launch restraint and commanded release | only a non-physical keepout envelope | HOLD |

No pad material, allowable pressure, friction coefficient, preload magnitude,
loss budget, fastener, release device or qualification cycle is selected.
Contact geometry alone does not establish launch restraint.  The trade cannot
be down-selected until the primary-structure seat is redesigned and formal
loads or an approved unit-load programme are available.
