Reaction Control Propulsion Module X13003000-01
• Inherently safe non-toxic R134a propellant
• Two seals against leakage of propellant
• Eight fast response, low power 25 mN cold 
gas thrusters
• Smart system with integral electronic con-
troller
VACCO’s Reaction Control Propulsion Module is a self-
contained unit designed to occupy the center of a 3U
CubeSat. A total of eight thrusters in groups of two at four
corners of the module allows for precise rendezvous and
proximity operations.
The Reaction Control Propulsion Module uses R134a
propellant, which self-pressurizes over the normal operat-
ing temperature range and helps to ensure range safety.
This smart system is designed to interface with the space-
craft through an RS422 data bus for command and con-
trol. 
The Reaction Control Propul sion Module can be scaled
down from 0.8U based upon the mission and payload
requirements.Operating Temperature Range................. 0°C to 60°C
Operating Voltage....................................9 to 12.6 vdc 
Max Standby Power Consumption..............  0.25 watts
Max Steady-State Power .................................. 5 watts

Nominal Continuous Thrust ................25 mN +/- 5 mN
Minimal Impulse Bit.................................. 0.20 mN-sec
Thruster Valve Response .............................. <2 mSec
