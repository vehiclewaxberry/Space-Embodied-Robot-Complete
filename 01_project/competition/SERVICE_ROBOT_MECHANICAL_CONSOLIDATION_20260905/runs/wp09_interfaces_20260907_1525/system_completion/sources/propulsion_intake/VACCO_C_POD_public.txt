VACCO’s Reaction Control Propulsion Module is a
self-contained unit designed to occupy the center of
a 3U CubeSat. A total of eight thrusters in groups of
two at four corners of the module allows for precise
rendezvous and proximity operations.
The Reaction Control Propulsion Module uses
R236fa propellant, which self-pressurizes over the
normal operating temperature range and helps to
ensure range safety. This smart system is designed
to interface with the spacecraft through an RS422
data bus for command and control. 
The Reaction Control Propulsion Module can be
scaled up or down from 0.8U based upon the mission
and payload requirements.
